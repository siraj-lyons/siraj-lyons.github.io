## Background and motivation

- Project investigated the role of information integration across the sensorimotor network and its connectedness with cognitive and affective networks while evaluating social threat. It was hypothesized that increased information integration between cognitive, affective, and sensorimotor networks would occur under conditions of heightened social threat. 

## Methodological contributions to the project

- Graph theoretic measures calculated in Matlab using the [Brain Connectivity Toolbox](https://sites.google.com/site/bctnet)
- Mass univariate analyses were completed in R
- Performed machine learning classification using Support Vector Machines with the [libsvm](https://www.csie.ntu.edu.tw/~cjlin/libsvm/) package


## Results

- Results from project are published in _Frontiers in Neuroscience_ by [Foley et al. (2026)]( https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2026.1782939)
